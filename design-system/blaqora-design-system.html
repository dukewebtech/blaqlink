Add the fonts and the two stylesheets, in this order. Every class starts with bq-, so it won't clash with Bootstrap, Tailwind or anything else you add later.

Never hard-code a colour, size or shadow. Reference the token so a change in one place updates everything.

Principles

When a decision isn't covered here, these five ideas settle it.

1

Obvious beats clever

A vendor should know what a screen does and where to tap within three seconds. Name things the way vendors talk: "Payouts", not "Settlement".

2

Money is sacred

Amounts are always exact, bold, tabular and never truncated. Fees are shown before they're charged.

3

Colour means something

Blue is action. Coral, violet, amber and green belong to the four ways to sell. Nothing is coloured just to decorate.

4

Calm, then one wow

Keep screens quiet and spacious, and spend boldness on one moment per view: a hero, a total, a confirmation.

5

Motion explains

Animate to show what changed or what just happened, like a sale arriving or a step completing. Never animate only for show.

6

Honest by default

No invented numbers, fake reviews or dark patterns. Empty states guide vendors to their next action.

Foundations

Logo

The icon and wordmark sit side by side with a 10px gap at nav size. Use the icon on its own for app icons, favicons and small spaces.

Reversed, on night surfaces

Clear space and size

Keep clear space equal to the height of the icon's inner "b" on every side. Minimum sizes: icon 24px, full lockup 96px wide.

Do

  • Use the supplied PNG files, or an SVG export of them
  • Use the blue wordmark on light surfaces and the white one on dark
  • Round the icon's corners at 28% of its width

Don't

  • Recolour the wordmark in category colours
  • Stretch, rotate, outline or add shadows to the wordmark
  • Use the old stacked-layers logo from the early dashboard

Foundations

Motion

Two curves and four durations cover everything. Press play to compare them. Every animation must switch off when the device asks for reduced motion.

Ease, --bq-ease

Default for entrances, panels, tabs and anything that moves across space.

Spring, --bq-spring

For small objects that pop in: toasts, confirmations, checkmarks, toggles.

TokenDurationUse for
--bq-dur-fast150msColour and background changes on hover
--bq-dur-base250msMenus, toggles, accordions, button press
--bq-dur-slow450msTab panels, screen changes, drawers
--bq-dur-enter800msPage-load hero sequence, scroll reveals

Do

  • Animate the result of a vendor action: sale received, item added, step done
  • Use one orchestrated moment per page, like the hero load
  • Animate only transform and opacity

Don't

  • Fade every section in on scroll
  • Animate money totals for longer than 400ms
  • Loop anything near a form a vendor is filling in
Foundations

Icons

Line icons on a 24px grid with 1.8px strokes, rounded caps and joins. They're stored as a sprite, so you reference each one by id. Click an icon to copy its code.

To get the icons, open this page's source and copy the hidden <svg> sprite at the top of the body into your page once. Then use the snippet you copied, for example <svg class="i"><use href="#i-wallet"/></svg>.

Guidelines

Accessibility checklist

Check every new screen against this list before shipping.

  • Text contrast is at least 4.5:1, or 3:1 for text over 24px
  • Tap targets are at least 44×44px
  • Every input has a visible label
  • Errors are announced (role="alert") and linked with aria-describedby
  • The visible focus ring (3px blue) is never removed
  • Everything works with a keyboard, including tabs with arrow keys and Esc to close menus
  • Reduced motion switches off loops and entrances
  • Colour is never the only signal; always pair it with a word or icon

Notes for version 1.0

Changes from the homepage build

The soft text colour moved from #7C83A3 to #636A8C so it passes AA contrast. Classes are now prefixed bq-. Danger colours and toggle, checkbox, select and alert components were added for the dashboard. The homepage will switch to these files when we build the next pages.

Moving to another framework

blaqora-tokens.json holds every value as plain data. Map it to a Tailwind theme, Flutter ThemeData or React Native StyleSheet so all platforms share one source of truth.
