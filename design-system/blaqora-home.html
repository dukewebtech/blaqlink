Questions vendors ask

Can't find your answer? Email hello@blaqora.store and we'll get back to you.

What can I sell on Blaqora?

Four things, from the same store: event tickets, bookings for your time or services, physical products, and digital products like e-books, templates and course files.

Do I need a website or a developer?

No. You get a ready-made store at your own blaqora.store link. Add your items, share the link, and you're selling.

How much does it cost?

₦4,999 a month, plus 3.5% and ₦100 on each sale. The ₦100 isn't charged on sales under ₦2,500. Everything is included in the one plan.

How do my customers pay?

They check out on your store through Paystack's secure payment page.

How do I get my money?

Your sales show up in your Blaqora balance. From Payouts in your dashboard, you withdraw to your bank account.

Can I sell more than one type of thing?

Yes. Sell tickets on Friday, take bookings on Saturday and sell your merch all week, from the same link.
